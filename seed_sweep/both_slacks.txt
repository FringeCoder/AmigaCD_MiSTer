Seed 1 is the one to ship. It clears the project's own criterion, both slacks
positive, with roughly three times the margin of the +0.039 seed 18 build that
prompted this sweep.

Worth noting for the next person: three of five seeds fail on HOLD, not setup,
and the setups are all comfortably positive. That matches what the #28 arms
showed -- hold violations scattering across seeds and across both arms of a
comparison, including arms carrying none of the change under test. Hold is the
placement-sensitive edge on this design; a single failing seed says nothing about
a change.

Not a shipping decision on its own. +0.110 is a modest margin and the only thing
that settles a build on this core is booting it. Compare the earlier seed 16
datum -- +0.152 and a black screen with no Kickstart -- but note that was an
UNCONSTRAINED build, where this interface's timing was unmodelled and plausibly
the cause. Since #30 the SDRAM interface carries 5.7 ns of setup margin and the
binding path is internal, so that datum does not transfer to a constrained one.
